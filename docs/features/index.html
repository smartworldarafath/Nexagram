Feature list

Enhancements Nexagram adds on top of official Telegram applications, folded by category; select a category to expand it. A check mark means the client implements it natively.

Shared across platforms

These features are implemented in Nexagram for Android, iOS and desktop. Entry points and details vary slightly by platform.

  • Multi-provider translation

    Choose Google, Microsoft, Yandex and other services, or connect your own LLM endpoint with a custom prompt.

  • Message filters

    Match messages by keyword or regular expression, then mask, collapse or hide them.

  • Repeat and forward without quote

    Repeat a message in one step, or forward it without the source.

  • Pangu spacing

    Insert spaces between CJK and Latin text or digits when sending, editing or reading.

  • Hide sponsored messages

    Sponsored messages in channels are no longer shown.

  • Settings backup and sync

    Export or sync your settings so a new device needs no reconfiguration.
