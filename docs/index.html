Key Enhancements

A glance at the extra capabilities Nexagram delivers over the official Telegram application.

  • Liquid Glass Optics & M3E

    Physics-based glass material with depth blur, interactive morphing, and Material 3 Expressive styling.

  • Forced Copy & Invert Reply

    Copy protected channel messages with ease, invert replies, and forward messages cleanly without source quotes.

  • Combine Message & Edit Style

    Combine multiple text chunks and media into a single bubble; customize typography and formatting options.

  • Multi-Provider & LLM Translation

    Translate chats with Google, Microsoft, Yandex, DeepL, or connect your private OpenAI/Claude LLM endpoints.

  • Built-in Universal Proxies

    Built-in WebSocket CDN relay, MTProxy with IPv6, Shadowsocks, VMess, and one-tap proxy subscription import.

  • Hardened Privacy & Zero Tracking

    No ads, sponsored channels automatically hidden, block unwanted secret chats, and zero third-party telemetry.
